Skymarshal 1.1 finds likes you could not see before.

When someone deletes their account or takes a post down, your like on it stays in your archive. Bluesky's feed skips those, so nothing could show them to you — including Skymarshal. The app now reads your archive directly, lists those likes with everything else, and deletes them like any other record.

Also new since 1.0.2:
- Word patterns in Insights: which words show up in your higher- and lower-engagement posts, worked out on your phone.
- Guided Mode can use larger controls, fewer choices per screen, two-step confirmations, and extra time.
- VoiceOver labels are clearer across content, account rows, widgets, charts, and media.
- Rate-limit pauses and interrupted deletes now explain what happened.
- Account signals and filter explanations use clearer language.
- The app icon has a new, simpler design.

Fixes:
- Background refresh no longer tries to load an entire large archive at once, so big accounts see fewer background crashes.
- Duplicate follows are collapsed before a scan, so the counts line up.
- Removed a Settings toggle that no longer controlled anything.

After real use across separate sessions, the app may occasionally invite an App Store rating. Never on first open, never mid-delete.

App password only. No off-device usage analytics or tracking. No Skymarshal server.
