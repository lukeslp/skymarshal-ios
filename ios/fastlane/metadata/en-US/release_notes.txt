Skymarshal 1.0.3 makes bulk cleanup easier to follow and safer to finish.

- Guided Mode can use larger controls, fewer choices per screen, two-step confirmations, and extra time.
- VoiceOver labels are clearer across content, account rows, widgets, charts, and media.
- Rate-limit pauses and interrupted deletes now explain what happened.
- Background graph refresh no longer tries to load entire large archives at once (fewer background crashes on big accounts).
- After real use across separate sessions, the app may occasionally invite an App Store rating. Never on first open, never mid-delete.

App password only. No off-device usage analytics or tracking. No Skymarshal server.
