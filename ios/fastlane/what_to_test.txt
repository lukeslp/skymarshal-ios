Build 53 — interactive visuals and the Bluesky constellation.

What to try:
1. Open Scope and choose Interactive Visuals.
2. Sign in to the web surface if prompted, then switch among Post Interactions, Network, Post Sentiment, Communities, and Constellation.
3. In Constellation, enter a Bluesky handle and choose Map Sky. Select accounts in the accessible explorer beneath the star map and open a profile.
4. Return to Scope and confirm the native Account Graph, Post Graph, Interactions, and Timeline Heatmap remain available.

The constellation is a first-party Skymarshal visualization built from the same follower-network and community data as the other network views. The native session is never injected into web content, and Open in Safari remains available as an alternative.
